The following describes the schema relationships and meanings for the datasets:


Cohort Overview:
- AMD and FMT datasets represent the healthy cohort.
- EMULSION dataset represents the diseased or unhealthy cohort related to NAFLD or what we call MASLD
- All tables prefixed with "response_" come from qualtrics survey data


EMULSION Cohort Tables:
- emulsion_amra_data → Body scan data for the Emulsion cohort.
- emulsion_clinical_metadata → Clinical metadata for the Emulsion cohort.
- emulsion_subject_ind_metadata → Stores indicators showing whether a subject has completed FFQ, AMRA scan, and sequencing.


Healthy Cohort (AMD/FMT) Survey Tables:
- ffq_grams_per_day → Food Frequency Questionnaire (FFQ) grams-per-day data.
- ffq_nutrients_per_day → FFQ nutrients-per-day data.
- ffq_servings_per_day → FFQ servings-per-day data.


All tables prefixed with `response_` represent survey-related data collected from AMD/FMT (healthy cohort):
  - response_ccmq → CCMQ questionnaire data.
  - response_demographic → Subject demographic data.
  - response_smoking → Smoking-related responses.
  - response_psqi → PSQI questionnaire data.
  - response_pss → Perceived Stress Scale (PSS) data.
  - response_physiology → Height, weight, and BMI data.
  - response_metadata → Contains response_id mapping to survey_id.
  - response_medical → Medical history data.
  - response_gpaq → GPAQ (Global Physical Activity Questionnaire) data.
  - response_dietary_habits → Dietary habits survey data.
  - response_score → Scoring or aggregated results from survey responses.


Other Related Tables:
- lilac_kit → Contains kit_id data.
- lilac_sequence / sequence_16s / sequence_shotgun → Sequence ID data.
- pre_ftp_raw / post_ftp_raw → FMT (Fecal Microbiota Transplant) data before and after treatment.
- fmt_sequence_mapping / ftp_sequence_mapping → Mapping tables linking FMT sequencing data to sample metadata.
